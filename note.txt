The Legend of Toba Lake

ORIENTATION
Once upon a time, in a lush village in North Sumatra, there lived a young man named Toba. Toba was a humble and hardworking farmer who lived alone. One day, while fishing in a nearby river, Toba caught an unusual golden fish. To his surprise, the fish spoke to him, pleading for its life. Toba, taken aback but compassionate, decided to release the fish back into the water.


The next day, a beautiful woman appeared at Toba's door. She revealed that she was the fish she had spared and, as a reward for her kindness, she had been transformed into a human. Toba and the woman fell in love and got married, but she warned him to never reveal her true identity as a fish to anyone. They lived happily and were blessed with a son named Samosir.
Years passed, and Samosir grew into a curious and playful boy. One day, when Samosir disobeyed his mother, Toba, in a fit of anger, blurted out the secret of his wife's true identity. "You behave like the son of a fish!" he shouted.

Upon hearing this, his wife wept bitterly. She knew that the secret was out, and the curse would now come into effect.
She instructed Samosir to run to the hills, as a great disaster was about to occur. She herself transformed back into a fish and disappeared into the river. Suddenly, heavy rain began to pour, and the village was flooded. The water kept rising, eventually forming a vast lake, which is now known as Lake Toba. The island in the middle of the lake is named after Samosir.


Today, Lake Toba stands as a reminder of Toba's mistakes and the love and sacrifice of the fish-woman. The legend of Toba Lake is told to remind people to keep their promises and to respect the ones they love.
